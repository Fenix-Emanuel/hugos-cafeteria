Our story

A place named after my dad.

My dad's name was Hugo. Our kitchen table was the center of everything: coffee in the morning, something sweet on the table, people coming and going. There was always room for one more.

When I moved to Texas, I brought that table with me. Specialty coffee done properly, the pastries I grew up eating, and a room that feels like somebody's home.
