About Us

A Place Named After My Dad

My dad's name was Hugo. Growing up, our kitchen table was always the center of everything — coffee brewing in the morning, something sweet on the table, people coming and going. He had this way of making everyone feel welcome, like there was always room for one more.

When I moved to Texas, I carried that with me. HUGO'S Cafetería is my way of keeping that table going: specialty coffee done properly, the pastries I grew up eating, and a room that feels like somebody's home.
